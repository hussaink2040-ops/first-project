## Brand & Style
The brand targets discerning, design-conscious consumers seeking sustainable luxury without ethical or aesthetic compromise. The visual tone balances architectural minimalism with organic warmth, projecting quiet confidence, artisanal precision, and forward-thinking material innovation. 

The aesthetic is grounded in high-fashion editorial publishing: generous, uncrowded compositions, deliberate pacing, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and museum-grade negative space. Physicality is expressed not through faux-skeuomorphism, but through tactile tonal shifts, whisper-thin hairlines, and large-format photographic framing.

## Colors
The palette evokes raw textiles, stone, and unbleached organic fibers:
- **Primary (`#121212`)**: Deep charcoal black used for core typography, authoritative structural accents, and high-emphasis primary actions.
- **Secondary (`#5C6352`)**: Muted botanical olive representing organic origins, used for conscious tags, secondary badges, and delicate accent indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#8C7B6D`)**: Warm earth clay providing grounding accents, subtle metadata, and textural balance.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5`)**: Natural unbleached ivory canvas providing an eye-resting foundation warmer than digital white.

Supportive neutrals include surface stepping at `#F5F2EB` (subtle containers), borders at `#E8E3DA` (structural gridlines), and muted text at `#757E69` / `#8C7B6D`. Never use pure digital black (`#000000`) or stark white (`#FFFFFF`) except within high-contrast photographic imagery.

## Typography
Headings rely on Playfair Display set at regular weights with tight, editorial leading to emulate print run headers and spread titles. Editorial italics should be reserved for poetic hooks and material origin notes. 

Body and interface microcopy utilize Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver crisp legibility and technical modernity. Use uppercase styling paired with tracked letter spacing (`0.12em` to `0.14em`) exclusively for functional tags, category metadata, navigation headers, and micro-labels to create an intentional juxtaposition against the serif headlines.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an asymmetrical 12-column desktop grid (6-column on tablet, 4-column on mobile) calibrated for luxury storytelling. Content relies heavily on breathing room, with vertical section breaks expanding up to `5rem` to `8rem` on larger displays. 

Columns feature structural hairline vertical rules when separating product specs or editorial notes. Content alignment alternates deliberately: left-aligned editorial captions flank full-bleed or offset imagery, avoiding dense, centered e-commerce blocks.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation rejects synthetic skeuomorphism and heavy drop shadows. Depth is communicated strictly through:
- **Tonal Layering**: Stacking `#FBF9F5` base surfaces over `#F5F2EB` and `#E8E3DA` containers.
- **Micro-Borders**: Crisp 1px borders in `#E8E3DA` or 15% opacity charcoal (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.08)`).
- **Ambient Veil**: For sticky navigation bars, quick-look drawers, and modals, use backdrop filters (`backdrop-blur: 16px`) over a 90% opaque ivory fill (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.90)`).
- **Float Elevation**: Elevated dialogs or floating cart summaries receive a wide, muted contact shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.06)`.

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly architectural, sharp, and razor-finished (`roundedness: 0`). Buttons, input frames, image tiles, modals, and product containers feature precise 90-degree right angles. This discipline references high-end architectural framing and editorial magazine blocks, avoiding playful or generic digital curves.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#121212` background, sharp edges (`0px`), text in `#FBF9F5`, styled in all-caps `label-md`. Hover triggers an invert transition to `#5C6352`.
- **Secondary/Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px hairline border in `#121212`, text in `#121212`. Hover fills with `#F5F2EB`.
- **Text Link**: Understated inline link with a persistent 1px baseline offset border that expands outward on hover.

### Form Inputs
- Single 1px underline or clean 4-sided hairline border in `#E8E3DA`.
- Background is transparent or `#F5F2EB`.
- Focus state shifts the border crisply to `#121212` without fuzzy focus rings.
- Floating or inline label set in uppercase `label-sm` with wide tracking.

### Cards & Editorial Lookbooks
- Zero border radius. No drop shadow.
- Enclosed by a 1px `#E8E3DA` border or simply floating within disciplined whitespace margins.
- Image aspect ratios are locked to classical portrait (3:4 or 4:5).
- Hover on product tiles initiates a subtle, slow image zoom (1.03x scale over 600ms easing) without moving card boundaries.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Crisp 1px square borders for checkboxes; sharp concentric circles for radio buttons.
- Checked state fills with solid `#121212` with sharp inner indicator.

### Tags & Chips
- Thin hairline border (`#DCD5C9`), no background or soft `#F5F2EB` tint.
- Typography set in `label-sm` uppercase.
- Sustainability markers feature an inline bullet point tinted in `#5C6352`.

### Storytelling Drawers & Accordions
- Minimalist hairline rules (`#E8E3DA`) dividing composition rows.
- Plus (`+`) and minus (`−`) glyph toggles in light weight, avoiding rounded chevron pills.
